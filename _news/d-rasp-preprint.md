We propose a way to rewrite trained models into D-RASP -- a programming language that mimics transformers, and show that it works, at least in the controlled setting of small models and algorithmic and formal languages tasks. See the paper on [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.08857) and [this blog post](https://lacoco-lab.github.io/home/decompiling_transformers) for more visualizations and examples of decompiled programs.

UPD: accepted to [ICML 2026](https://icml.cc)!
